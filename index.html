About

Classical resources for contemporary ethical life

My research focuses on Confucian philosophy from pre-Qin thought to Song-Ming Neo-Confucianism, with particular attention to moral cultivation, ethical subjectivity, judgment, responsibility, and the contemporary transformation of classical Confucian resources in the age of AI and digital media.

I am especially interested in how Confucian traditions can illuminate questions of agency, education, moral formation, and practical responsibility across historical and comparative contexts.
